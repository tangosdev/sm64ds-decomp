# started spawning: hearts, coins, stars, the timer and the camera buttons, all

# its factory is the CONSTRUCTOR at 0x020fe154 -- the ROM stores the ctor itself

# CALLEE_SEAM because the port's LoadFile decodes on the way out while the
# body decodes its result itself -- hal/hud_load.cpp is the raw-loader seam.
# port/unmatched/HUD_InitResources.cpp, the hand copy that carried both by
# hand, is retired. See status/HUDINITRES.md.
