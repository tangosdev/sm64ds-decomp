1. THE SHELLS DID NOT COLLIDE.  FIXED.
================================================================================

WHAT IT WAS. dScMgCurling_c has exactly two functions that move one shell out
of another shell's way, and BOTH of them were return-0 faces in
hal/scene_mg_faces.cpp. Neither has a delink block in
config/arm9/overlays/ov006/delinks.txt and neither has a src TU, so the port
had refused both and the shells slid through one another.

  func_ov006_020e1dc8  size 0x2f4  the SHOT-TIME separation. Called once per
                                   shot by src/func_ov006_020e2c08.c, the
                                   matched release handler, right after it
                                   writes the shot shell's heading and speed.
                                   Un-stacks whatever the shot shell is already
                                   overlapping, one level of cascade deep.
  func_ov006_020e20bc  size 0x5e0  the PER-FRAME collision. Called by
                                   src/func_ov006_020e2868.c, the matched
                                   physics tick, every frame for every shell it
                                   ticks, and again through the veneer
                                   func_ov006_020e285c for shells at rest. This
                                   is the one that carries the physics: equal-
                                   mass elastic exchange along the contact
                                   normal, new heading and speed for both
                                   shells, de-penetration, rink clamp, clack.

THE BRIEF EXPECTED ONE OF THESE TO BE THE TINT PASS. IT IS NOT. Both are
physics and the disassembly settles it without needing the symptoms: neither
touches a GX register, a polygon list, a palette or a display-control word.
Their entire callee set is three functions -- cstd::sqrt, cstd::atan2 and
func_02012718 (a sound) -- and their entire data reference is data_02082214,
the arm9 angle table. There is nothing in either body that could draw anything.

WHAT WAS DONE. Both transcribed from the ROM as host copies:
    port/unmatched/MgCurling_Collide_020e1dc8.cpp
    port/unmatched/MgCurling_Collide_020e20bc.cpp
under port_ names (port_mg_curling_collide_020e1dc8 / _020e20bc), the same
naming ruling CT1 applied to port_mg_curling_st_020e1854 and the port applied
before that to port_player_st_climb_main: the decomp has no body for either
address and nothing in this tree may spell one as if it did. The two faces in
hal/scene_mg_faces.cpp now forward to them. Full derivation, literal pools,
field tables and the four ROM oddities that were transcribed rather than tidied
are in the two files' own banners and are not repeated here.

THE BASE WAS PROVED BEFORE ANYTHING WAS READ. extracted/overlays/overlay_0006.bin
at 0x020bfec0, checked three ways against this pair's own relocation rows:

ONE THING BROKE AND WAS FIXED IN THE SAME CHANGE, AND THE FIRST FIX FOR IT WAS
WRONG. src/func_ov006_020e285c.c is a twelve-byte ARM tail-call veneer to
020e20bc whose src declares itself and its callee `(void)`.
unmatched/MgCurling_StateDispatch.cpp calls it with no arguments and its header
argues, correctly, that this is the harmless ARM ride-through class because the
callee ignores its arguments. That stopped being true the moment 020e20bc
became a real body.

The first attempt hosted the veneer with named riders and commented its src TU
out of port/slice_mg1.txt. THE GATE REFUSED IT: linkage fell 5831 -> 5830,
because that TU was one of the 5831 and taking a matched TU out of the link to
replace it with a host copy is a coverage loss however faithful the copy is.
Reverted.

What is there instead is the mechanism the port already built for this exact
shape. MSVC compiles a one-call forwarder as a JMP, reusing the caller's own
cdecl frame, so the two arguments the dispatch pushes are still at [esp+4] and
[esp+8] when the collision body reads them -- the same ride func_0204f76c gives
func_0205ac5c and roughly fifty ov007 rows depend on. The matched src TU stays
in the link untouched, the dispatch declares 020e285c as (char *, int) and
passes (c, a), and port/tools/tailjump_guard.py gets a CLASS C row that fails
the build if MSVC ever emits a call there. The dispatch header carries the
amendment.

2. THE SOUND COMMANDS.  FIXED -- AND THEY ARE NOT THE MUSIC.
================================================================================

WHAT THE LOG SAID:
    [snd] command 0x04 parameter 0x0a not implemented -- ignored
    [snd] command 0x04 parameter 0x0c not implemented -- ignored

WHAT THEY ARE. Command 0x04 is TRACK_PARAM and it has exactly ONE emitter in
the whole decomp: src/func_0205a8f0.c, a one-liner. Five matched TUs call it
and each pins one parameter to one constant, which is the entire vocabulary:

    func_0205ac5c  param 0x19  size 1
    func_0205ac84  param 0x1a  size 1
    func_0205acac  param 0x09  size 1   pan        (was already hosted)
    func_0205acfc  param 0x0a  size 2   THIS LANE
    func_0205acd4  param 0x0c  size 2   THIS LANE

  0x0a IS AN ATTENUATION IN TENTHS OF A DECIBEL. Its only ARM9 feeder,
  src/func_0204f82c.c, does not pass a number through: it passes
  data_02086384[idx]. That symbol is 0x100 bytes of arm9 rodata (symbols.txt
  puts data_02086384 at 0x02086384 and the next symbol at 0x02086484) and
  reading it out of extracted/arm9_dec.bin at base 0x02004000 gives 128 s16:
      -723 -421 -361 -325 -300 ... -3 -3 -2 -1 -1 0
  monotone, ending at 0, bottoming at -723. It is the NITRO volume-to-decibel
  table. The proof it is being USED as one is that src/func_0204fafc.c builds
  PLAYER_PARAM 6 by summing three lookups into the same array and clamping to
  -0x2d3, which is -723. So 0x0a is player param 6's per-track twin: same
  table, same units, same clamp, one track instead of all of them.

  0x0c IS A PITCH OFFSET IN 1/64 OF A SEMITONE. Two matched callers fix the
  scale between them. src/func_ov007_020bdbcc.c sweeps
      -(((0x1000 - t) * 0x300) >> 12)
  so full deflection is -0x300 = -768 units, and 768 units to the octave is
  what port/hal/sdat/sseq.cpp's own bend arithmetic already works in.
  src/func_02048af4.c sends (height - 0x28) * 2 from the 3D sound updater.

WHERE THEY COME FROM IN CURLING. Both are sent by the 3D positional sound
updater beside the pan the port already had -- src/func_02012860.c sends all
three together on flag bits 1, 2 and 4 -- and curling's rolling-shell loop is a
3D positional sound (src/func_ov006_020e2868.c ends by refreshing a looping
handle through func_02012468). So what was being dropped is the shell sound's
distance attenuation and its pitch, every frame, for every rolling shell.

WHAT WAS DONE. Track volDb10 and pitch added to the sequencer's per-track
state, applied at note start and to notes ALREADY SOUNDING (both parameters are
re-sent every frame, which is the whole point of them), with the track mask
honoured rather than assumed to be track 0. sd_mix_set_rate was added for the
pitch, so a retune cannot drag the level and pan along with it, and the three
places that recompute a sounding voice's level now go through one helper. Files:
port/hal/sdat/sseq.cpp, mixer.cpp, consumer.cpp, sdat.h.

WHY THIS IS NOT THE MUSIC, AND THE BRIEF SAID IT WAS. Three independent
reasons, any one of which is enough:

  a. A BGM layer is started by command 0x00 and nothing else. No matched TU
     sends 0x04 for a music layer.
  b. Both parameters are TRIMS. They adjust a sequence that is already
     playing. Dropping them cannot stop one from starting.
  c. The level path and the minigame path start music through THE SAME
     FUNCTION, _ZN5Sound22LoadAndSetMusic_Layer1Ei. hal/star_flow.cpp calls it
     for a course and src/func_ov004_020ae330.c calls it for a minigame. The
     brief's guess that curling uses "a different player/group start variant"
     does not survive reading either caller.

REGRESSION EVIDENCE FOR THE LEVEL PATH. There is no audio gate in the battery,
so this is the honest form of the guard rather than a green: the change adds
two `else if` arms to a switch that previously fell through to a print, and
touches no other opcode and no other parameter. A level boot's command stream
is unchanged because it never sends 0x04 with 0x0a or 0x0c -- the emitters are
the 3D updater and the ov007 pair, neither of which runs on a course boot's
music start -- and both new setters return immediately for an inactive player.
The full battery is green either side of the change with delta 0, which covers
everything about the sound layer except the part nothing in this repo can
measure yet.

NOT FIXED HERE, AND NOT GUESSED AT. The brief assigned this to one of the two
ov006 bodies. Section 1 refutes that from the disassembly: both are physics and

THE CANDIDATE IS THE THIRD LOG LINE, func_0202e78c, and it is a defect
whatever it turns out to draw:

    [scene] UNMATCHED ov004/ov006 body entered: func_0202e78c

  * 0x0202e78c IS NOT AN ADDRESS WITH NO SYMBOL. config/arm9/symbols.txt:1116
    reads `_ZN2GX15DisableAllBanksEv kind:function(arm,size=0x48)
    addr:0x0202e78c`, and src/_ZN2GX15DisableAllBanksEv.c is a matched body
    that turns off all thirteen VRAM banks. The face's own comment ("an arm9
    address with no config symbol at all") is simply wrong, and
    port/mg_fanout_costs.txt:907 and port/nfs_names_map.txt:373 repeat it.
  * So this is a NAME-SPELLING hole, not a missing body. src/func_ov004_020b2980.c
    is a twelve-byte ov004 veneer spelling the same address func_0202e78c
    (config/arm9/overlays/ov004/relocs.txt:526 `from:0x020b2988 kind:load
    to:0x0202e78c module:main`), the port stubbed that spelling, and the real
    body was never called. hal/scene_boot.cpp:1182 already aliases a THIRD
    spelling of the same function, the MSVC-mangled one, so the tree carries
    two working routes to it and one dead stub.
  * WHY IT IS A PLAUSIBLE TINT. Its one caller, src/func_ov004_020b265c.c, is
    the minigame's VRAM setup: set graphics mode, DISABLE ALL BANKS, then
    re-assign BG/OBJ/sub-BG/sub-OBJ and decompress tiles into 0x6404000 and
    0x6604000. Skipping the teardown leaves the previous scene's bank
    assignments live underneath the new ones, which is exactly the shape of "a
    layer that should be there is not".
  * WHY THIS LANE DID NOT JUST FIX IT. Two reasons, and the second is the real
    one. First, the brief scoped it out on the grounds that the same trap fired
    in working sessions -- which is weak evidence (a session with no tint is
    also a session where this fired). Second and decisively: NOBODY HAS LOOKED
    AT A FRAME. Routing a stub to its real body is a two-line change and it is
    the wrong two lines to make blind, because DisableAllBanks running where it
    did not before changes what every 2D layer in the scene reads. That wants a
    before/after capture and the owner's eye on it, which is the standing rule
    for anything visual.

THE OTHER CANDIDATE, and it is not exclusive with the first: the port's 2D unit
has no blend hardware. port/ntr/include/ntr/ppu.h says so twice -- "the
blend/window units are not implemented" and "No BLDCNT blending" -- and a light
blue wash over a shadow is what a semi-transparent layer looks like. If the
tint is a BLDCNT alpha blend it cannot appear no matter what runs, and the fix
is in the PPU, not in ov006. Deciding between the two is one capture with the
scene's BLDCNT/BLDALPHA writes logged, which is a small job for whoever owns
port/ntr.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP: capture scene 374 before and after routing func_0202e78c
to _ZN2GX15DisableAllBanksEv, and log the scene's writes to 0x04000050..0x04000054
in the same run. That answers both candidates at once. It is deliberately not
done here.
