# data_ov019_021135d8 and data_ov019_021135cc are the two bss statics
# __sinit_ov019_02112b14 (this class's own sinit, matched src) writes and
# IceSlideManager::InitResources reads back; emitted zeroed here, seeded by the
# sinit at boot (hal/actor_overlays.cpp). The OTHER sinit,
# __sinit_ov019_021127a4, is RacingPenguin's (259, not spawned by level 11) and
